HOW IT WORKS

A tiny decision maker

Put one option on each line. FunHub chooses one option at random when you press Pick one. It is useful for small everyday decisions, games, study groups or deciding what to do next.

How many options can I add?

The picker accepts up to 100 non-empty options.

Is the result truly random?

The page uses the browser’s random number generator to select an option from the list.
