HOW IT WORKS

Simple conversions in your browser

Choose a measurement category, enter a number, select the two units and FunHub converts the value locally. Temperature uses Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin; length and weight use common everyday units.

Does the unit converter need an account?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and does not require an account.

Which units are included?

Length includes millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, inches, feet, yards and miles. Weight includes grams, kilograms, ounces and pounds.
