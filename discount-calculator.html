HOW IT WORKS

Calculate a sale price

The discount amount is original price × discount percentage. The sale price is the original price minus that discount amount. The result is calculated locally in your browser.

Can I use decimals?

Yes. Enter prices and discount percentages with decimals if needed.

Does this calculate tax?

No. It calculates the discount only; taxes, delivery fees and other charges are not included.
