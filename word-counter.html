HOW IT WORKS

What the counts mean

Words are counted by groups of non-whitespace characters. Characters include spaces; the no-spaces count excludes whitespace. Lines count each non-empty line, with an empty text box showing zero.

What is the maximum text length?

This page accepts up to 20,000 characters to keep the tool fast on phones and browsers.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The counter processes the text in the page. FunHub does not send the text itself to Google Analytics.
